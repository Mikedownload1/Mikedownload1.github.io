[arXiv261005] ServeTwin: A Benchmark-Validated Simulator for Distributed LLM Architecture Exploration

arXiv

Authors
Sungjoon Park, Changue Jung, Kyungno Joo, Mincheol Kang, Jaehyung Ahn, Sehwan Lee, Sangjoon Kim
Institution
Samsung Advanced Institute of Technology

The paper presents ServeTwin, a closed-loop simulator that combines specification-driven analytical timing with a stateful serving loop to model distributed LLM serving, including prefill-decode disaggregation and multi-turn agent workloads without target-hardware profiling. Validated against real deployments, it reproduces InferenceX's throughput-interactivity frontier with 3.6% mean error and predicts multi-turn performance with 9.9% error. Pre-silicon sweeps show that the preferred HBM bandwidth-capacity tradeoff reverses across workload states and that higher concurrency shifts the bottleneck from memory bandwidth to scheduler and runtime overheads.

The paper presents LOGOS, an unsupervised system that uses entity-event co-occurrence and temporal precedence to collapse millions of raw log lines into a compact precedence forest for incident triage.
