Authors

Institution

This Systematization of Knowledge paper unifies KV cache optimization research by deriving closed-form arithmetic intensity for decode-phase attention and parameterizing it across NVIDIA H100, B200, and AMD MI300X hardware. Evaluating one representative method each from quantization, token eviction, KV paging, prefix caching, and heterogeneous tiering at 128k context, it identifies a three-regime structure where weight traffic dominates below a hardware-specific crossover context length and KV traffic dominates above it, with each compression domain trading measurable quality for bandwidth savings.
