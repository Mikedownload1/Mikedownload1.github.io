[arXiv261012] Capturing Electrical Windows Through Reconfigurable Optical Networks: Transfer-aware Scheduling of Multi-Region AI Data Centers

arXiv
syscluster infrastructureoptical circuit switching, transfer-aware scheduling, compute-electricity-network coordination, AI data centers, demand-side management, load flexibility, checkpoint transmission, renewable energy utilization
Authors
Xi Tong, Zijian Li, Xiaoyi Fan, Cong Zhang, Ershun Du, Qin Wang
Institution
The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, Fudan University, Tsinghua University, Sichuan Energy Internet Research Institute

The paper proposes a transfer-aware compute-electricity-network scheduling framework that coordinates training placement, checkpoint transmission, and optical circuit switching to improve renewable energy utilization and reduce grid-purchase costs in multi-region AI data centers. By accounting for transfer delays and dynamically scheduling optical circuits, the method achieves a 12.50% reduction in real-time grid purchase cost and a 2.724 percentage point increase in local renewable energy utilization compared to static optical operation. The approach is particularly beneficial when renewable-rich or low-price windows shift across sites and network bottlenecks hinder timely checkpoint delivery.

[arXiv261012] SlackSwap: Bandwidth-Aware Activation Swapping and Recomputation for Memory-Efficient DNN Training

arXiv
mlsysllm trainingactivation swapping, recomputation/rematerialization, host-link bandwidth budget, FIFO offload, earliest-deadline-first prefetch, checkpointing, memory-efficient DNN training
Authors
Hui Feng, Xun Zhou, Yuxin Wang
Institution
University of California Merced, University of Texas at Austin, Independent Researcher

SlackSwap treats host-link time as an explicit bandwidth budget, spending it on activations that are most expensive to recompute and recomputing the cheapest remaining tensors, with FIFO offloads and earliest-deadline-first prefetches scheduled without per-layer barriers. In trace-driven simulations of BERT-Large, GPT-2 XL, and a Llama-7B-style model on an A100, it retains 96–100% of unlimited-memory throughput at half the activation memory, beating the best baseline by 1.10–1.11x and staying within 0.5% of a genetic search while planning in under 0.4 seconds.
